Daily System

Your AI Learning Routine

A structured daily practice that builds compound knowledge. Consistency is the only variable.

6 STEPS
1
☀️ Morning · 10 minutes

Current Affairs Scan

  • Open Perplexity AI — ask "Top 3 India news today"
  • Ask "Key pharma or chemical news this week"
  • Ask "Key geopolitical developments today"
  • Scan Exploding Topics for 2 min for new trends
2
📚 Study Block · 30 minutes

Deep Learning with Claude

  • Pick one chemistry, pharma, or geopolitics topic
  • Ask Claude for mechanism + industry link
  • Follow up: "Why? What are the implications?"
  • Save the key insight to Google NotebookLM
3
🔬 Research Block · 20 minutes

Academic Deep Dive

  • Use Elicit.org to search for academic papers
  • Upload interesting PDF to NotebookLM
  • Ask 3 specific questions about the paper
  • Cross-verify claims on Consensus.app
4
🌍 Evening · 15 minutes

Big Picture Thinking

  • Ask Claude: "What are India's 2030 risks?"
  • Check Metaculus for expert probability forecasts
  • Reflect: How does this connect to my field?
  • Write one key insight in a physical notebook
5
💡 Weekly · 1 Hour

Synthesis & Foresight Session

  • Run a Claude or Perplexity Deep Research task
  • Pick one large domain topic for the week
  • Build a structured note across 5 sources
  • Teach it to someone — best retention method
6
📊 Monthly · Knowledge Audit

Review & Plan

  • Review all saved NotebookLM notes this month
  • Identify which sectors changed most
  • Update your mental model of the world
  • Choose next month's primary focus domains
Core Method

How to Use AI Like a Master

The 3-layer mechanism and mental models that build genuine understanding.

3-LAYER METHOD
1
Layer 1 — Retrieve

Search & Find

Perplexity AI, Elicit, Semantic Scholar. Replace hours of manual searching with cited, accurate answers in seconds. Always anchor to facts first.

2
Layer 2 — Reason

Analyse & Connect

Claude for long-form reasoning. NotebookLM to interrogate PDFs and reports. Connect chemistry, business, and geopolitics into one coherent mental model.

3
Layer 3 — Synthesise

Forecast & Create

Claude or Perplexity Deep Research. Multi-source synthesis into comprehensive insight — from cancer mechanisms to India's 2035 strategic position.

The Follow-Up Rule

The first answer is never the best. Always ask: "Why?" — "What does the data show?" — "Counterarguments?" — "India implications?" Understanding is built through iteration.

The Chemistry Advantage

Your chemical thinking — mechanisms, cause-and-effect, reaction pathways — applies perfectly to geopolitics, business, and society. Every system has a mechanism.

Depth Over Speed

Social media trains you for speed. AI gives depth — only if you ask deep questions. Shallow prompts produce shallow answers. The quality of your question IS your thinking.

Build a Second Brain

Use NotebookLM as your personal vault. Upload everything important. Chat with it monthly. Compounded knowledge is an unfair advantage that grows every day.

The Gen Z Contrast

Most people your age consume 5–7 hrs of short-form video daily, degrading deep focus capacity. One hour of AI-guided learning per day creates a 10-year knowledge gap in your favour.

Cross-Domain Thinking

Biggest insights come from connecting different domains: India's water crisis → agrochemical demand → API supply chains → geopolitics. AI makes these connections fast.
